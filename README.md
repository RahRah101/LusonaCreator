# LusonaCreator

An open, offline-friendly way to draw, share and animate *sona* (lusona), the sand-drawing tradition of the Chokwe people of Angola.

**Status:** early. The sona file format comes first; editor and animations after.

Why it exists and how it's designed: [devnotes/Blog_draft.md](devnotes/Blog_draft.md)

---

## What LusonaCreator is

Sona (singular *lusona*) are the drawings of the Chokwe, Luchazi and neighbouring peoples of Angola and nearby regions: lines drawn around arrangements of dots, often used as mnemonic devices to tell stories, proverbs and lessons. LusonaCreator digitizes and revives them. 

Its heart is an **open file format** that describes a lusona (its dots, its lines, the order they're drawn in, the story that goes with them, and where it was recorded) in plain text any program can read. Around the format sit **generators** that render and animate a sona, a **catalogue** of real recorded tusona with their sources, an **editor** to make new ones, and a **scanner** that turns a photo of a sand drawing into an editable file, offline.
