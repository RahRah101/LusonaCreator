# Sources to get

The primary collections of sona: the people who actually recorded them in the field. Everything Gerdes draws comes from these (his captions say "[Fontinha, p. 134]" etc.). 

## Primary collections (most new drawings)

| Priority | Reference | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fontinha, Mário (1983), *Desenhos na areia dos Quiocos do Nordeste de Angola*, Instituto de Investigação Científica Tropical, Lisboa, 304 p. | **The big one.** The book that started Gerdes' work. Most of his figures cite it. Hundreds of drawings with names and meanings |
| 2 | Pearson, Emil (1977), *People of the Aurora*, Beta Books, San Diego, 168 p. | Ngangela sona collected by a missionary over ~50 years. Source of Figs. 326–327 and the *cadeira* |
| 3 | Santos, Eduardo dos (1961), pictograms and ideograms of the Quiocos (full title not in the extracted bibliography: check) | Source of Figs. 324–325, the multilinear ones |
| 3 | Santos, Eduardo dos (1960), "Sobre a matemática dos Quiocos de Angola", *Garcia de Orta*, Lisboa, 8, p. 257–271 | Article; ILL |
| 4 | Kubik, Gerhard (1987b), "African space/time concepts and the tusona ideographs in Luchazi culture…", *African Music*, Grahamstown, 6(4), p. 53–89 | Kubik on tusona, beyond the book we have |
| 4 | Kubik, Gerhard (1987a), "African graphical systems", *Muntu* (CICIBA), Libreville, 4, p. 71–135 | |
| 5 | Hamelberger, E. (1951), "Écrit sur le sable", *Annales Spirituelles*, Paris, 61, p. 123–127; (1952), "A escrita na areia", *Portugal em África*, Lisboa, 53, p. 323–330 | Early missionary records |
| 5 | Centner, Th. (1963), *L'enfant africain et ses jeux… au Katanga*, CEPSI, Elisabethville (p. 218–228) | Sona as children's games, Congo side |
| 5 | Baumann, Hermann (1935), *Lunda: bei Bauern und Jägern in Inner-Angola*, Berlin | Earliest ethnographic record |
| ? | Nange Kudita wa Sessemba's manuscript (1970s) | A Cokwe anthropologist's own collection. Unpublished as of 2012, per Gerdes. If it ever surfaced, it'd be gold |

## Gerdes' other books

- *Sona Geometry from Angola: Mathematics of an African Tradition* (Polimetrica, 2006): the English edition of the book we have in Portuguese.
- The trilogy's volumes 2 (sona in education and research) and 3 (comparison with Egypt, Mesopotamia, Tamil kolam, Vanuatu, Celtic knots), published in French (1995) and German (1997).
- *Geometry from Africa: Mathematical and Educational Explorations* (MAA, 1999).
- Gerdes & Djebbar (2007), *Mathematics in African History and Cultures: An Annotated Bibliography*: the place to find more sources.
